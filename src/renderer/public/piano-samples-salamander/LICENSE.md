These piano samples (2 velocity layers — "soft" and "bright" — sparsely sampled every major third:
A0, C/D#/F#/A per octave, pitch-shifted to fill in between) are repackaged MP3s of the
[Salamander Grand Piano](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3) by Alexander Holm.

- Repackaging/loader code ([darosh/samples-piano-mp3](https://github.com/darosh/samples-piano-mp3)):

- Original recordings (Salamander Grand Piano V3): [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
  (c) Alexander Holm

"soft" uses the velocity3 layer (quiet dynamic), "bright" uses the velocity15 layer (loud, more
percussive dynamic) — the same instrument recorded at different striking force, giving genuinely
different timbre (not a synthesized EQ/filter difference).
